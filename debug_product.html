Celebrate every bite.

Make fluffy confetti cupcakes or cake with nostalgic cake batter flavor and a pop of color from dye-free sprinkles. This quick, no-mixer-needed mix is made with Supernatural® sprinkles, colored with vibrant, plant-based ingredients instead of synthetic dyes. It’s an easy way to bring a little extra fun to birthdays, celebrations, or any day that calls for something cheerful.

Makes:

  • Makes 12 cupcakes, two 6” round layers, or one 8” round layer
  • Six 4" Whoopie Pies
  • 18 Cake Pops
